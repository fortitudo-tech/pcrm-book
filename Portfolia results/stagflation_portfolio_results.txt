RISK CONSIDERATIONS
======================================================================

RISKS TO BE AWARE OF:

1. Interest Rate Sensitivity
   - Both VNQ and TIP sensitive to rate changes
   - Rising rates = falling REIT prices initially
   - TIPS have duration risk (prices fall when rates rise)
   - Mitigation: Income generation offsets some price decline

2. Opportunity Cost Risk
   - If equities boom, you'll miss out significantly
   - This is DEFENSIVE, not growth-oriented
   - Sacrificing upside for downside protection
   - Only appropriate if you believe stagflation likely

3. Concentration Risk
   - 78% in single sector (Real Estate)
   - Vulnerable to housing market corrections
   - Consider geographic exposure of VNQ holdings
   - Not diversified across asset classes

4. Inflation Forecast Risk
   - Based on assumption inflation remains elevated
   - If inflation falls quickly, TIPS underperform regular bonds
   - If deflation, strategy could underperform
   - Past inflation ≠ future inflation

5. Historical Data Limitations
   - 2015-2025 had limited stagflation (except 2021-2022)
   - Optimization based on recent "Goldilocks" era
   - True 1970s-style stagflation data not in sample
   - Results may not reflect actual stagflation performance

MITIGATIONS:
- 22% TIPS provides stability anchor
- Semi-annual rebalancing maintains discipline
- REITs provide income even if prices stagnate
- Low CVaR limits worst-case losses
- Can adjust if scenario changes

======================================================================
TAX CONSIDERATIONS
======================================================================

REIT TAXATION (VNQ):
- Distributions taxed as ordinary income (not qualified dividends)
- Typically higher tax burden than equity dividends
- Consider holding in IRA/401k if possible
- Reduces after-tax returns in taxable accounts

TIPS TAXATION (TIP):
- Interest payments taxed as ordinary income
- Principal adjustments (inflation) also taxable annually
- "Phantom income" issue - taxed on gains you don't receive yet
- Also better in tax-advantaged accounts

TAX EFFICIENCY RANKING:
1. Best: Hold both VNQ and TIP in IRA/Roth IRA
2. Okay: Tax-deferred 401k/403b
3. Worst: Taxable brokerage account

If in taxable account:
- Consider municipal bonds instead of TIPS
- Look at direct real estate (depreciation benefits)
- Account for 20-30% tax drag on returns
